<objective>
Establish the durable, refresh-safe storage for the day cell's descriptive flavor (MANDATORY / PTO) as a nullable `day_off_type` column on the existing `agent_day_hours` table (Phase 9), per the D-12 research resolution. This is the storage foundation the parser (plan 03) writes into.

Purpose: Recurring MANDATORY/PTO must block the solver for a weekday forever and must NOT be silently wiped by `BambooRefreshService`'s rolling-window `AgentDayOff` delete-and-regenerate. Storing dated `AgentDayOff` rows (the literal reading of D-05) hits that window-wipe hazard; `agent_day_hours` is queried without a date range and a `0.00` row already durably blocks the solver via the unmodified `resolveEffectiveHours` chain — the new column is reporting/label metadata only, read by nothing solver-side.
Output: Flyway V30 migration, the `dayOffType` field on `AgentDayHours`, and a regression test proving refresh never touches `agent_day_hours`.
</objective>
