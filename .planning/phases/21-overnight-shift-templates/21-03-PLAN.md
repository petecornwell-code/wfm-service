<objective>
Give the violation payload typed date and time fields, and move the backend's string parser onto
them — without touching the label's text.

Purpose: the operator-facing timeslot label is load-bearing as DATA, not just as text. Two
independent parsers split it on its first space to recover a date and a time —
`ScheduleExportService.unfilledSeatsByDateAndSlot` and the allocation grid in
`ScheduleResults.tsx`. OVNT-07 has to change that label's shape to disclose a calendar span, so the
fields must land and both parsers must move first. One of those parsers is also wrong today in a
way nothing reports: it keys unfilled seats by the label's CALENDAR date while the allocation sheet
looks them up by the agent-day's BUSINESS date, so on a non-midnight-anchored desk a seat unfilled
at 02:00 lands under a key no sheet ever asks for.

Output: four typed fields on `ViolationDetail`, populated at both construction sites, with the
export's parser reading them. The label text is deliberately unchanged — 21-10 changes it, after
21-08 moves the frontend parser.
</objective>
