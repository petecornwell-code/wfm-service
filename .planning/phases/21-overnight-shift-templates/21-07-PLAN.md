<objective>
Make the Excel export render an overnight shift as one thing, and say which calendar days it
spans.

Purpose: OVNT-06's literal text asks for "a continuation indicator on the morning-after cell", and
on the Roster sheet that premise is void — `ScheduleOutputService` keys the agent schedule on
business date, so a `22:00`-`06:00` shift on a `21:00`-anchored desk already occupies exactly one
column, and business day D+1 does not begin until calendar D+1 at 21:00, long after the shift
ended. There is no morning-after cell there to annotate. What the Roster cell genuinely lacks is
the disclosure OVNT-07 asks for, and the operator chose its form from three rendered alternatives.

Real fragmentation lives on the per-date Allocation sheet, which writes one sheet per date with
columns in clock order. A `22:00`-`06:00` span therefore renders as two runs of cells at opposite
ends of the row — the two fragments OVNT-06 forbids.

Output: a roster cell that spells out both weekdays, a vertical legend carrying its new row, and an
Allocation sheet ordered from the desk's day start in both dimensions.
</objective>
