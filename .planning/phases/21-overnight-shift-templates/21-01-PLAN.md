<objective>
Make an overnight shift template genuinely creatable, and make the refusal on a desk that cannot
hold one tell the truth about why.

Purpose: this is the phase's spine. Every other plan in Phase 21 renders, validates, scores or
discloses an overnight template; none of them can be proven against anything until one can be
saved. The acceptance path already works on an anchored desk (`anchoredIsForwardWithinDay` maps
`22:00-06:00` to offsets 60 -> 540 at a 21:00 anchor), so the production work here is the refusal
message (D-02) and a fixed maximum span (D-08) — plus discharging the inherited registry entry that
currently asserts the opposite.

Output: `ShiftTemplateService.validate` refuses an anchor-crossing pair by naming the anchor,
refuses a span over 16 hours by naming the span, and accepts a real overnight envelope with correct
net hours — with the OVNT-01 asserts-todays-behaviour registry entry removed in the same change
that flips its assertion.
</objective>

## Assumption-delta checkpoint (phase-wide, advisory)

The deterministic detector fired `pluralization` on this phase's roadmap section. Re-reading the
scope confirms a real generalization, though not the one the matched word pointed at: this phase is
where a desk's business day stops being the same thing as a calendar day *for an operator* — the
model landed in Phases 18 to 20, but no reachable desk could exercise it until 21-02 and 21-08 make
a non-midnight anchor creatable.

- **Primary noun:** the **business day**, identified by a business date plus an offset from the
  desk's day-start anchor. The calendar date is demoted to a detail of one variant — the variant
  where the anchor happens to be midnight, which is every desk that exists today.
- **Decision: `promote`.** Every surface this phase touches moves to the generalized
  representation as its primary, unconditionally, with the calendar date retained only where an
  operator genuinely needs it to find a row on a calendar (the violation label, and the span
  disclosures in 21-07 and 21-10 — all of which name it explicitly rather than implying it).
  Nothing is added alongside a still-required clock-ordered path: 21-06 replaces the solver's
