<objective>
Give OVNT-05 a check with teeth, shared by every path that can introduce or admit a template.

Purpose: OVNT-05 as written — "refuses an overnight template whose envelope does not fit inside its
desk's business day" — is near-vacuous. At any anchor every start/end pair maps to offsets inside
`[0, 1440]`, so once the forward-interval test passes the envelope fits the business day by
construction. The check with teeth is containment inside the desk's real generated operating
window, `[bounds.startTime(), bounds.endTime()]` — and `TimeslotBoundsResponse.endTime()` has zero
call sites in either validation service today, which is exactly the v1.3 gap this requirement
inherits. An overnight envelope reaching past the desk's last generated slot is seats that do not
exist.

Output: one containment predicate, called from the save path, from the shift-library report, from
the SHIFT-mode gate and from candidate generation. Blocking for overnight templates, where no
desk has one today so the regression risk is nil; advisory for same-day ones, because `dev` is the
live system and a blocking check there could strand a stored row.
</objective>
