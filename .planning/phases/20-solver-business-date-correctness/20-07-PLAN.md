<objective>
Move the one destructive write path in this phase, with its range and its filter in the same edit, and prove
the deleted span by observing which rows survive rather than by reasoning about a query.

Purpose: the demand-upload replace path derives a min and max date from the payload's timeslots and hands
them to a delete whose backing query filters on the timeslot's calendar date. On a re-anchored desk that
deletes the wrong span of live demand — it destroys operator data rather than mis-scoring a schedule, which
makes it the most damaging thing available anywhere in this phase. It also sits on the literal demand-upload
path SOLV-07 names, so excluding it would let the guard's green overstate what SOLV-07 proves. Research
flagged the hazard precisely: the range and the filter must move together, and a half-migration here is
worse than none.

Output: a business-date-filtering delete method, the replace path's range derived from business dates and
passed only to that method, observed-survivor tests at two anchors, and the join guard finally green.
</objective>
