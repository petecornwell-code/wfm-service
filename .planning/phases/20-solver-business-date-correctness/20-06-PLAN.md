<objective>
Make coverage reporting and shift-library generation resolve the same business date for a timeslot that the
solver now resolves, so SOLV-07's "provably the same date" claim covers the read paths and not just the
solver.

Purpose: SOLV-07 names three consumers — demand upload, coverage reporting and the solver. The solver moved
in plan 20-05. Coverage reporting buckets a timeslot by calendar date in six key positions, so on a
re-anchored desk a post-midnight slot is reported against the wrong day. Shift-library generation is worse
than mis-reported: it derives a weekday from the calendar date, so on a 21:00-anchored desk a 02:00 slot
belonging to business-day Monday has calendar weekday Tuesday and demand is bucketed under the wrong
weekday entirely — a real instance of this defect class, surfaced in discussion rather than inherited.

Output: ten key-position edits across two services, the two display labels deliberately unchanged and
documented, and assertions at a 21:00 anchor proving both services now follow the business day.
</objective>
