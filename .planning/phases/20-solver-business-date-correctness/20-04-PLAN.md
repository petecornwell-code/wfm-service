<objective>
Close the two defects that are not joins: a SLOT-mode stretch crossing midnight charged to the wrong
days, and a pre-solve gate that would refuse every shift-mode solve on a re-anchored desk.

Purpose: SOLV-04's defect is reachable on today's data and independent of overnight templates — the
timeslot on an assignment is fixed and the agent is the only planning variable, nothing forbids the same
agent either side of midnight, and the contracted-hours constraints group by calendar date. SOLV-05's
defect is sharper than a mis-keyed report: the gate's shift-assignment map is keyed by business date and
its timeslot map by calendar date, so on a re-anchored desk the lookup silently returns empty, supply
computes to zero, and a solvable desk is refused. A false refusal, not a wrong number.

Output: a constructed SLOT-mode fixture that fails today and passes after the migration, and the gate's
key-system mismatch fixed with both directions of its behaviour asserted at a non-midnight anchor.
