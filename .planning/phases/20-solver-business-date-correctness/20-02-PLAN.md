resolves a timeslot's calendar date where business date is meant, and one that fails if the derived
business date on `AgentDayConfig`/`AgentShiftAssignment` ever stops being derived.

`business_date` column on `agent_shift_assignment` for a derivation chain — which is only safe if the
chain is structurally enforced. Both guards land before the migrations they police, and each carries a
proof that it can actually fail.

Output: `BusinessDateJoinGuardTest` with an empty allowlist, two liveness proofs and five documented
scope boundaries, red until the last of the four guarded files is migrated; and
`AgentDayDerivationGuardTest`, green from the first commit.
