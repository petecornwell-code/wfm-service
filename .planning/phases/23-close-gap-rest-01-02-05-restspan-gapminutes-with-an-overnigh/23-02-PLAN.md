**PF-02 — `RestSpan.ofSlots` cannot emit a span that wraps past the anchor, from grid-aligned data.**

`23-RESEARCH.md` Finding 2 states that a historical SLOT-mode predecessor span "genuinely can wrap".
Re-derived from `RestSpan.ofSlots` (lines 76-96) during planning: `ofSlots` orders the agent-day's
slots by `anchoredStartMinute` and takes the first slot's start and the last slot's end. For the
resulting span to wrap past the anchor, an INDIVIDUAL timeslot must itself cross the anchor — which
grid generation from the day start does not produce. A set of slots crossing calendar midnight at a
non-midnight anchor does not wrap relative to that anchor, and at a `00:00` anchor a business date's
slots are confined to that calendar day.

Consequence, and why it does not descope anything: the SLOT-mode wrap is reachable through a
pre-horizon `RestSpan` supplied as a problem fact to the constraint stream and as a `List<RestSpan>`
parameter to `requireRestFeasibility` — which is exactly where 23-01 Task 3 and this plan's Task 1 and
Task 2 pin it, with directly-constructed spans, matching the existing fixtures' own idiom. D-01 is
honoured in full: both call sites are corrected and both are proven. What changes is only the SLOT case
in `RestPredecessorServiceTest`: instead of forcing an unreachable wrapping fixture there, Task 3
asserts the crossing-midnight-but-not-the-anchor case is numerically UNCHANGED — a real regression
guarantee rather than a manufactured one. Record PF-02 in the SUMMARY; 23-03's registry file states it
as a scope boundary.
