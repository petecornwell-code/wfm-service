<objective>
Close the D4 half of G-15-10: the report layer derives BOTH the displayed shift envelope and the
displayed breaks from the seat pattern, so it structurally cannot render an envelope violation as a
violation — it silently redraws the envelope around the violating seat.

`ScheduleOutputService` computes `shiftStart`/`shiftEnd` as min/max over the agent-day's own seats
(:174-175) and `breaks` as every discontinuity between consecutive held seats (:191, body :465-478,
whose signature accepts only `List&lt;AgentAssignment&gt;` so it structurally cannot read the band).
Meanwhile the authoritative `ShiftDescriptor` — template name, envelope start/end, band offset and
band duration — is resolved at :433-450 from the real `AgentShiftAssignment` and then discarded.
That is why the same agent-day reads "Late 12:00-21:00" in the Agent Allocation group header
(which uses the authoritative values) and "09:00-21:00" in the Agent Schedule table. Two views of
one fact disagree, and the disagreement IS the violation, surfaced as one nowhere. The mislabelling
propagates into `ScheduleExportService`'s Break rows and into the preference report's
`actualBreakTime` / `breakTimeHonouredCount` KPIs.

Per operator ruling OR-2 this ships alongside the solver fix, not after it: the reporting defect is
what let this reach UAT undetected, and shipping the solver fix alone would leave it verified with
an instrument known to hide the exact failure it is meant to detect.

Purpose: make the report layer read the authoritative facts D-07 already denormalises and D-04
already carries on the live pair, and make divergence between the true envelope and the actual
seats a first-class, visible fact instead of an absorbed one.
Output: authoritative shift spans and band-derived breaks in shift mode, a divergence surface on
every shift-mode agent-day entry, corrected export and preference KPIs, and a regression test that
inverts the report-layer characterisation.

**Not in this plan:** any solver or constraint change; any change to slot-mode reporting; any change
to the Agent Allocation grouping (`ScheduleResults.tsx:632` already reads the authoritative values
correctly and is not the defect).
</objective>
