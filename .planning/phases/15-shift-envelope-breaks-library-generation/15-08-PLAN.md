<objective>
Measure the shift model's effect on schedule quality against the slot model honestly, with the
threshold committed before the run, and answer the construction-heuristic ordering question by
measurement rather than by convention.

Purpose: Phase 12 produced a +0.25h median inside a 5.00h noise spread, read it as an improvement,
and was withdrawn. XCUT-04 exists so that cannot happen twice. `SPIKE-COUPLING.md` already predicts a
soft-quality plateau on the sound Option A coupling; the operator ruling is to ship the sound model,
measure the real gap at realistic scale, and report it as a finding — not to remedy it, and not to
discover it at UAT.

Output: `15-BENCHMARK.md` carrying a threshold written down before the run, per-run numbers, a
verbatim PASS or FAIL verdict, the named plateau finding, the CH-ordering winner, and a piloting
recommendation.
</objective>
