<objective>
Make the seat-supply gate's supply NUMBER trustworthy, and stop its refusal text recommending the
most destructive available action on this desk.

Two independent defects sharing one method, both with a written-down fix.

G-15-21 — `requireShiftEnvelopeSeatSupply` computes `coveredTimeslots` TWICE (around lines 1184 and
1281) with the calendar-blind predicate `pairs.stream().anyMatch(p -> p.covers(ts))` over the
DESK-WIDE pair list. `ShiftBandPair.covers(Timeslot)` compares only envelope and break TIMES. So on a
weekend the gate counts hours only a Mon-Fri template reaches and OVER-COUNTS library supply — the
exact failure its own javadoc warns of ("wave through an unsolvable one if it over-counts"). This is
the third site of a class already fixed twice this phase; the two definitions of "covered" diverged
when `expandMinimumStaffingSeats` became date-aware in 6c82241 and this did not. The live symptom is
the advisory reading "tightest at 08:00-09:00 with 0 seat(s)", which is incoherent on its face.

G-15-24 — the refusal text offers raising the desk's over-allocation limit as its FIRST suggestion.
The live desk overrides `unassignedAssignmentWeight` from the source default `ofSoft(1000)` to
`ofHard(10000)`, and seat COUNT scales with the over-allocation ceiling, so on this desk each
manufactured seat no agent can fill is worth 10,000 HARD. The gate has no knowledge of that weight
and cannot know its own advice is unsafe.

Purpose: one date-aware definition of covered supply, and advice the gate has actually checked.

Output: a shared date-aware coverage helper used by both sites, a weight-aware refusal message, a
red-proof that the weekend over-count is gone, a no-false-refusal measurement, and the live-weights
rule written down where the next analyst will hit it.
</objective>
