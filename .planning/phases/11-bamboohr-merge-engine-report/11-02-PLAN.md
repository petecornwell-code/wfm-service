<objective>
Close the two requirements plan 01 left open, and with them the D-05 one-way door plan 01 deliberately
deferred to a sibling plan.

MRG-06: an agent whose week BambooHR does not know but the spreadsheet does becomes solver-eligible,
and stays that way — a persisted provenance marker stops a routine BambooHR refresh from silently
downgrading them back out of the solve (D-15), and the Upload Results modal announces exactly who
crossed that line this upload (D-14).

MRG-03: BambooHR's dated PTO governs every date inside its synced window, so a date it holds no PTO
record for is a working day even where the sheet's recurring weekly PTO says otherwise; outside that
window the sheet's recurring pattern fills (D-09). The same solve-time pass carries D-05's un-blocking
— a weekday the sheet marks as worked is no longer blocked by a stale BambooHR field-4517 MANDATORY
row — and nothing new is persisted to achieve either (D-10).

Purpose: MRG-03 and MRG-06 are the two places where the merge changes *who gets scheduled* rather than
what a record says. Both rest on one architectural choice — arbitration at solve time rather than at
merge-write time — which is a one-way door, so it is gated by a decision checkpoint before it is built.

Output: a `V36` provenance migration and its enum, a downgrade guard in the refresh path, a
newly-eligible callout in the Upload Results modal, two new static arbitration helpers in
`SolverService`, a `Working pattern (Mon–Sun)` merge-report row, and five new test classes.
</objective>
