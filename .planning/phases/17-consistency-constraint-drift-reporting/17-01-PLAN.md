<objective>
Prove the whole consistency-and-drift architecture on one thin, production-quality path: a single
agent-day whose assigned shift envelope start differs from that agent's era-resolved usual shift,
penalised by a new soft solver constraint and reported by the drift report — both reading the same
distance function.

Purpose: this phase's two genuinely new pieces of architecture are (a) pre-solve era resolution fed
to the solver as a problem fact, and (b) DRFT-03's one-calculation-two-callers rule. Both are cheap
to get wrong and expensive to unpick after four more plans have been built on top. This plan wires
them end to end first, on the executor's best early-context tokens, and proves them with a runnable
test that asserts the constraint's penalty and the report's delta agree.

Output: V48 migration, the `ResolvedUsualShiftTarget` problem fact, `ShiftBandPair.startDeviationMinutes`,
the `Usual shift consistency` constraint, `buildDriftReport`, the `DriftReport` DTO family, and two
new test classes.
</objective>

**Decision: `no-change`.** The matched phrase is the requirement's own prohibition against a duplicate,
and the design already places the calculation on the model class rather than in either caller —
mirroring `ShiftBandPair.covers(...)`'s existing static-overload precedent in the same file. There is
nothing to promote: the primary was never a caller.

schedule with one drifted agent-day and asserts, in a single test, that the `usualShiftConsistency`
penalty magnitude and the `DriftReportEntry.deltaMinutes` for that day are both derived from the same
`ShiftBandPair.startDeviationMinutes` value. This satisfies DRFT-03's "not a second implementation"
requirement and the assumption-delta companion suggestion with one assertion, not two.
