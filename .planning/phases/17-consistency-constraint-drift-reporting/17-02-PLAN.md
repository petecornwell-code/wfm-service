<objective>
Make the precedence between usual shift and agent preference a stated, checkable invariant rather
than something a reader has to infer from relative weights, and put both new weights and the
tolerance band on the existing per-desk config API.

Purpose: CONS-06 rejects "implicit in relative constraint weights a reader would have to
reverse-engineer". A lower weight on its own *is* relative weights. What converts it into an
invariant is the save-time rejection plus a test, and what makes it observable is two separate lines
in `SolutionManager.explain()`. This plan builds all three of D-10's backend artefacts and the
enforcement that makes CONS-04's softness structural.

Output: the `Preferred start (shift mode)` constraint, save-time validation in
`ConstraintWeightsService`, three new fields on `ConstraintWeightsDto`, and four new test classes —
two of which (`ConstraintWeightsServiceTest`, `ConstraintWeightsControllerTest`) do not exist
anywhere in the repo and must be built from scratch.
</objective>
