<objective>
Retire the transitional one-argument `ShiftBandPair.covers(Timeslot)` form the tracer left in place,
and re-point its remaining five call sites across four services onto a window bound from a real
anchor.

Purpose: the tracer kept a midnight-delegating one-argument form specifically so it would not break
ten callers in the same commit as the class-shape change. That form is the one substitution D-07 warns
about — it compiles, it is correct on today's data, and it is indistinguishable at a call site from a
deliberate anchor. Deleting it is what turns "every caller supplies an anchor" from a convention into
a compile-time fact.

Output: two `covers` shapes, both taking a `DayWindow`, both reaching one implementation, and no
caller anywhere that can omit the anchor.

The phase's decision record lives in `19-01-PLAN.md`: `<planner_decisions>` P-01 through P-06 and
`<artifacts_this_phase_produces>`.
</objective>

<anchor_source_rule>
Apply P-02 from `19-01-PLAN.md` § planner_decisions at every call site in this plan, in order:
(1) the method already receives a `LocalTime dayStart` — bind it; (2) the class holds a
`DeskRepository` and the method has a `deskId` — load the `Desk` and bind `getDayStart()`;
(3) the method receives a `Schedule` — bind `getScheduleConfig().dayStart()`; (4) the lambda has
`cfg` in its tuple — bind `cfg.dayStart()`; (5) none of those, but a caller one level up satisfies
1–4 — add a `DayWindow window` parameter and let the compiler propagate; (6) propagation would reach
a controller or a `main` — bind `LocalTime.MIDNIGHT` and add a D-07 allowlist entry in plan 19-07.
