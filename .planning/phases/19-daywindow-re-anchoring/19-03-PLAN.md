<objective>
Expand the tracer's `DayWindow`-as-a-parameter convention across the three model helpers that do
interval arithmetic (D-09) and every one of their callers, and give `ShiftTemplateController` an
anchor source it can legitimately reach (P-03).

Purpose: these three helpers are the widest blast radius in the phase — roughly twenty production
call sites and fifteen test call sites — and they are the only place where the anchor has to cross
from a service into a model class. Getting the parameter type right here is what makes a transposed
anchor a compile error everywhere downstream.

Output: three model signatures carrying a `DayWindow`, every caller supplying one from a real source,
and the tracer's transitional one-argument `covers` form gone.
</objective>
