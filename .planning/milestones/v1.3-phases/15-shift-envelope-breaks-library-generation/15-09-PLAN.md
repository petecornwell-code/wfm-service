<objective>
Close the D2 half of G-15-10: make minimum-staffing seat supply envelope-aware on a SHIFT desk, and
make the test suite structurally capable of catching it if it regresses.

Two changes, one behavioural and one to the instrument that failed to see it:

1. **Seat suppression + in-envelope guarantee.** `SolverService.expandMinimumStaffingSeats` is
   structurally envelope-blind: its parameter list carries no `SchedulingMode`, no `ShiftTemplate`
   and no `ShiftBandPair`, so it manufactures one fillable seat on every timeslot regardless of
   whether any shift envelope reaches it. Per operator ruling OR-1 ("the library is the intent"),
   on a shift desk an hour the library does not reach is an hour the operator DECIDED not to staff,
   and it gets no seat. Conversely, an hour the library DOES reach but the forecast does not must
   get enough seats for everyone the library puts there — because the D-04 value range pins
   `netHours == effectiveHours` exactly, an agent must occupy 100% of their legal slots (D-05: one
   row per working agent-day), so a legal slot with no seat is an unsatisfiable obligation.

2. **The fixture that could not fail.** `ShiftModeFixtures` gives every template the ENTIRE
   operating window (:58-59) so an out-of-envelope timeslot cannot be constructed, and never calls
   `expandMinimumStaffingSeats` (:245) so production's filler seats are absent from every shift
   test. That is why 505 green tests and a 4-agent benchmark missed this: the shape is excluded by
   construction, not by size. Fixing it is mandatory — without it the regression returns silently.

Purpose: remove the out-of-envelope destination AND the in-envelope shortage at zero-demand hours,
so the cheapest-hard-violation arbitrage (D3) has nothing left to arbitrage at those hours.
Output: a shift-aware seat expansion, a fixture that can express the defect shape, and two
regression tests that fail against today's source and pass against the fixed source.

**Explicitly NOT in this plan:** raising `shiftEnvelopeComplianceWeight` (empirically refuted — it
relocates the residual onto `Contracted hours (under)` at 100x the cost), restoring any mode-gated
break constraint, and relaxing the D-04 exact-equality net-hours filter. Slot-mode behaviour is
unchanged; that is a hard requirement of this plan, not a hope.
</objective>
