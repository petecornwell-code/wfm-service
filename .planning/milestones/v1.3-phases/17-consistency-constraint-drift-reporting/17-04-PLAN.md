<objective>
Decide the shipped weight by measurement rather than inheritance, and only then write it.

Purpose: `consistent_start_weight` already exists on dev at `0hard/2soft`, and V38's own migration
comment justifies that number with arithmetic for a **per-agent** penalty — 28 CSRs times four
increments times two equals 224 soft, "enough to break ties between equal-coverage schedules, not
enough to outbid bulk under-allocation or minimum staffing". D-02 charges **per agent-day**. Same
desk, same weight, five working days, and the total lands above `minStaffingWeight`'s 1000, which is
consistency buying uncovered hours — the exact outcome that comment was written to prevent.
Inheriting the 2 unexamined is this phase's headline hazard, and this plan is the gate that catches
it before it ships.

Output: `17-BENCHMARK.md` with its threshold committed before any result, a gated seeded A/B harness,
a human decision on the shipped defaults, and the V49 migration that writes them.
</objective>

Constraint on the decision, load-bearing and not negotiable by the benchmark's numbers alone:
`ConstraintWeightsService` rejects a save where the preference weight's soft component is greater
than or equal to the consistency weight's. Both shipped defaults must therefore be non-zero soft
values with the preference weight strictly lower — a consistency default of 1 soft would force the
preference weight to 0 and ship that constraint disabled, which is not an acceptable outcome of this
plan.
