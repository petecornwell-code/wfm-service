<objective>
Make a waiver impossible to hide behind.

Two sections on the schedule's output — applied and unused — each entry naming the agent, both
business dates, both shift instants, the measured gap, the required gap and the recorded reason. Plus
two counts on the summary the schedule page already polls every two seconds.

Purpose: REST-07 exists so a waiver cannot silently hide a roster problem, and D-09 adds the mirror
image — a waiver pile nobody prunes. Both halves need the same surface, and that surface cannot be
the solver's score-explanation channel for two independent reasons. A waived pair that the solver
treats as legal produces no match at all, so there is nothing there to report even on the live path;
and the accepted path deliberately never consults that channel, building its violation report from
persisted rows instead. That second gap is the one a prior phase already hit, and this plan must not
repeat it.

The counts on the summary are load-bearing rather than a convenience: the detail payload is roughly
4 MB and competes with the solver for its two cores, so it must never be polled. A disclosure that
lived only in detail would be invisible to the operator it is for.

Output: `RestWaiverDisclosure` and `RestWaiverEntry` on the detail response, two counts on the
summary, `buildRestWaiverDisclosure` serving both paths, the accepted-path loader extension, and the
test class that pins all of it.
</objective>
