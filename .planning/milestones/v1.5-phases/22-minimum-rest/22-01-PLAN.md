<objective>
Prove the whole minimum-rest architecture end to end on one thin path, then give an operator the
entry point that drives it.

One desk sets a minimum rest; the value is stored on `desk`, snapshotted onto `schedule`, carried to
the solver as `ScheduleConfig`'s 15th component, and read by one new hard constraint that penalises
an agent whose shift on business date D starts too soon after their shift on D-1. A desk that sets
nothing produces no tuples at all.

Purpose: this is the phase's tracer. Every later plan expands outward from the path proven here — the
SLOT-mode constraint (22-02), the waiver exclusion (22-05), the horizon-edge lookback (22-06), the
pre-solve refusal (22-07) and the disclosure (22-08) all widen this slice rather than re-architecting
it. The genuinely novel code shape in this phase is the cross-business-date self-join and its anchored
gap arithmetic (22-PATTERNS.md records "No close self-join analog"), so it is proven first, on the
executor's best early-context tokens.

Output: V55 carrying all five DDL changes for the phase, `Desk.minimumRestMinutes`,
`Schedule.minimumRestMinutes`, `ScheduleConfig`'s 15th component plus a fourth delegating
constructor, `RestSpan` with the single gap-minutes implementation,
`ConstraintWeights.minimumRestShiftWeight`, the `Minimum rest (shift)` constraint,
`PUT /desks/{deskId}/minimum-rest`, and the two test classes that pin all of it.
</objective>
