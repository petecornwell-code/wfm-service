<objective>
Fix the defect end to end on one thin path, then close its sibling at the second call site.

One new wrap-aware primitive lands on `DayWindow`; `RestSpan.gapMinutes` is switched onto it; and one
overnight-predecessor fixture proves the whole path — overnight shift template on business date D-1,
through `RestSpan.ofShift`, through the `minimumRestShift` constraint stream, to a hard penalty that
did not exist before. Then `SolverService.requireRestFeasibility`'s SLOT pre-horizon branch is pointed
at the same primitive, deleting its hand-rolled duplicate of the formula.

Purpose: this is the phase's tracer. The sequencing is not cosmetic — the research's Finding 2 exists
because the gap formula was inlined a second time "by analogy" and drifted into the identical bug. The
primitive therefore lands and is proven BEFORE either call site is switched onto it, so there is
exactly one place to get a wrapped end offset from and the two sites cannot diverge again (D-01, D-08).
Plans 23-02 and 23-03 expand outward from this slice: 23-02 adds the remaining fixture coverage across
four more test classes, 23-03 adds the structural guard that makes a THIRD occurrence unrepresentable.

Output: `DayWindow.anchoredWrappedEndMinute(LocalTime, LocalTime)`; a corrected `RestSpan.gapMinutes`;
a corrected `requireRestFeasibility` SLOT pre-horizon branch; and new cases in `DayWindowTest`,
`MinimumRestShiftConstraintTest` and `RestFeasibilityRefusalTest` that are red before the fix and green
after it.
