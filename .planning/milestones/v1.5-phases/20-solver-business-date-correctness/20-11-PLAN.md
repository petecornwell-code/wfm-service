<objective>
Discharge advisory 1 of `20-VERIFICATION.md` — "is CR-02 the only remaining instance, or one of
several?" — by enumerating every `Timeslot`-receiver date read in `SolverService`, closing the two
that this planning pass found beyond CR-02, and writing the audit and the guard-scope decision down
where the next reader will meet them.

Purpose: the verifier flagged that `BusinessDateJoinGuardTest` does not scan `SolverService` at all,
that this is precisely how CR-02 survived, and that the residual risk is the undrawn area — a
~1900-line date-sensitive file with no structural guard, in a phase whose own review pass had
already demonstrated that one reading can miss instances. Planning answered the question directly by
enumerating the file. **CR-02 was not the only instance.** Two further `Timeslot`-receiver calendar-date
reads sit inside `runPreSolveValidation`, both comparing against values that are business-day-shaped
by `18-CONTEXT.md` D-22:

- `SolverService.java:1091` collects the timeslots' calendar dates into a set, then walks the
  schedule period — a business-date range — asserting each day is present. On a desk whose business
  day carries no timeslot on the matching calendar date (an anchor of 21:00 with an operating window
  wholly after midnight), the set misses that day and the solve is refused with a missing-timeslots
  error naming a day whose timeslots exist.
- `SolverService.java:1109` selects "the last timeslot on the same day as the first" by calendar
  date and compares its end time against the schedule's end time. On a business day that ends after
  midnight the selected row is the last one before midnight, so the comparison reports a spurious
  end-time mismatch.

Both are the same key-system class as CR-02, both are latent only while every desk is anchored at
`00:00`, and both become live refusals of a legal desk the moment one is re-anchored — the same
false-refusal shape `requireShiftEnvelopeSeatSupply`'s own comment describes.

Output: both reads resolved against the business date and proven at a 21:00 anchor; the file's
complete `Timeslot`-date enumeration recorded; and the decision to keep `SolverService` outside the
join guard's scope recorded with the measurement that justifies it.

**Scope boundary.** This plan does not change, widen or extend `BusinessDateJoinGuardTest`. Its two
prohibitions above state why, and `20-10-PLAN.md`'s `<advisory_dispositions>` carries the full
measured argument.

## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| persisted desk anchor → pre-solve validation | A desk's stored day start determines each timeslot's business date; pre-solve validation decides whether a solve is allowed to start at all. No untrusted input crosses here — the boundary is between stored state and an availability decision. |
| pre-solve refusal → operator | A refusal is the only signal an operator receives; a spurious one makes a legal desk unsolvable with a message that names the wrong cause. |
