<objective>
Change the label's text now that nothing parses it, and make the two planning documents say what
is true.

Purpose: two things were deliberately held until both parsers moved. The label itself —
`ScheduleOutputService` builds it by concatenating a calendar date and a time range at two places,
and until 21-03 and 21-10 landed, the Excel export and the schedule grid each recovered data from
it by splitting on its first space. Both now read structured fields, so the text can safely
disclose the span OVNT-07 asks for.

The other is a document amendment. OVNT-06 and the ROADMAP's fourth success criterion both ask for
"a distinct, non-blank, non-duplicate continuation indicator on the morning-after cell". On the
Roster sheet that premise is void: the agent schedule is keyed on business date, so an overnight
shift already occupies exactly one cell and there is no morning-after cell to annotate. The
requirement was written before any desk could hold an overnight template, so the falsity was
undiscoverable until this phase measured it. Real fragmentation lives on the per-date slot grids,
which 21-07 and 21-10 fixed.

Output: a label that discloses its span, and two documents that no longer ask for a cell nothing
produces.
</objective>
