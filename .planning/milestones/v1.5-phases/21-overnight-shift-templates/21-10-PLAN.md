<objective>
Convert the schedule grid onto anchored time, so an overnight shift renders as one thing.

Purpose: seven sites in this file compare, sort or subtract HH:MM strings with no notion of the
desk's day start. None of them throws. Each produces a plausible-looking wrong render: break slots
collapse to one, the full-day grid regenerates zero columns on a wrapping window, post-midnight
slots sort before evening ones so a single shift appears as two fragments, an overnight envelope
matches nothing so every hour renders as uncovered, and shift groups order by clock rather than by
position in the business day. They were all written when every desk was gated to a midnight anchor,
and this file has no test coverage of any kind, so none has ever been exercised against a real
anchor.

21-09 landed the anchor on the payload and a branded-offset module. This plan routes the page
through it — which turns each of the seven into a build error until it is converted — and adds the
business-day disclosure its section headings lack.

Output: a grid that is correct on a night desk and byte-identical on every desk that exists today.
</objective>
