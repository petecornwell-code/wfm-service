<objective>
Build the midnight-crossing scenario this milestone has never had, use it to prove two solver
scans are wrong on it, fix them, and close out the three inherited registry entries.

Purpose: Phase 18 deferred three boundary scenarios — a shift starting before and ending after
midnight, PTO on the starting versus the ending day, and the starting-weekday-only half of
contracted hours — because no desk could hold an overnight template. 21-01 makes one creatable, so
they are constructible here, and the registry that recorded the deferral is what makes the flips
falsifiable.

Building that scenario also exposes something no decision in this phase names. Phase 20's own
fixture javadoc flagged it and handed it here: the contiguity constraint's hole scan collects
assigned start times into a plain clock-ordered set and then reads its first and last. On a
`21:00`-anchored desk whose seats run `22:00` through `05:00`, the clock-first is `00:00` (anchored
minute 180) and the clock-last is `23:00` (anchored minute 120), so the scan range is
`[180, 180)` — zero iterations. The constraint reports no holes for a day that has one, and the
break scan on the same shape returns no break at all. Nothing fails; the score is simply wrong in
the generous direction. That is the silent-disagreement class this milestone exists to close, on
the one capability it exists to ship.

Output: a registered midnight-crossing scenario, two anchored scans, an empty registry.
</objective>
